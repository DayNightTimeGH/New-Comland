Colandian Law

These are the laws (regla) of New Comland. They apply inside the micronation and its official spaces. New Comland is a micronation, so real-world laws always come first.

Founding Principles
  1. New Comland was founded on July 29, 2026 by Day_Night_time and cjmario2.
  2. Day_Night_time runs the micronation, and cjmario2 helps run it.
  3. Real-world laws and the rules of any platform used by citizens come first. No Colandian law may require anyone to break them.
  4. New Comland must never claim to be a recognized sovereign state or to speak for any real-world government.
  5. Everyone is treated with respect, whether or not they are a citizen.
  6. Anyone may browse this website without being a citizen.
  7. The leaders may add, change or remove laws, and the current laws are published on this page.
Citizenship
  1. Citizenship is optional and is requested through the official application form.
  2. Applicants must give truthful information.
  3. Each person may hold only one New Comland citizenship.
  4. Citizenship of New Comland does not change anyone’s real-world citizenship or legal status.
  5. A citizen may give up their citizenship at any time by telling the leaders.
  6. The leaders may take away citizenship for serious or repeated law-breaking, after the citizen has had a chance to respond.
  7. Visitors and foreigners are welcome and follow the same laws while in New Comland’s official spaces.
Conduct
  1. Be kind and respectful to everyone.
  2. Harassment, bullying, threats and hate speech are not allowed.
  3. Nobody may be treated unfairly because of their race, religion, gender, sexuality, disability or background.
  4. Nobody may pretend to be a leader, official or another citizen.
  5. Nobody may share another person’s private information without their permission.
  6. Spam, scams and fraud are not allowed.
  7. Everything shared in official New Comland spaces must be suitable for all ages.
  8. Nobody may damage, vandalize or misuse the website or other shared property of New Comland.
  9. Disagreements should be settled peacefully, and if that fails they should be brought to the leaders.
Everyday Laws
  1. Citizens of New Comland are expected to follow the same everyday rules as real-world law, and the laws below spell out the most important ones.
  2. No stealing. Taking something that belongs to someone else without their permission is forbidden.
  3. No shoplifting. Pay for everything you take from shops and businesses.
  4. No littering. Put trash in a bin or take it with you.
  5. No vandalism or graffiti on property that isn’t yours.
  6. No violence. Hurting other people is forbidden, except to protect yourself or others from immediate harm.
  7. No trespassing. Don’t enter private property without permission.
  8. If you find lost property, return it to its owner or hand it to someone who can.
  9. Follow traffic rules and drive, ride or cycle safely. Never drive while impaired.
  10. No using or selling illegal drugs, and follow the real-world rules about alcohol and age limits.
  11. No cruelty to animals, and pick up after your pets.
  12. Protect the environment. Don’t pollute water, damage nature or start fires carelessly.
  13. Don’t copy or steal other people’s work, music, art or code and pass it off as your own.
  14. Pay for the goods, fares and services you receive, and pay back what you owe.
  15. Don’t follow, stalk, spy on or secretly record people.
  16. Be considerate with noise, especially at night.
  17. Respect the rules of shops, schools, workplaces and other public places.
  18. Treat police and emergency workers respectfully and follow their lawful instructions.
  19. If someone is in danger, call emergency services and help if you can do so safely.
  20. Breaking one of these laws in the real world is handled by the real-world authorities. New Comland does not protect anyone from real-world law.
Government & Justice
  1. The leaders make the decisions for New Comland and must act honestly and in its best interest.
  2. Citizens may suggest new laws and changes to the leaders at any time.
  3. The leaders may appoint law keepers (regla-homi) to help enforce the laws and settle disputes.
  4. Nobody may be punished for something that was not against the law when they did it.
  5. Anyone accused of breaking a law has the right to hear the accusation and respond before any punishment.
  6. Punishments must fit the offense. Minor offenses get a warning first.
  7. Possible punishments are a warning, a temporary suspension, loss of privileges, or loss of citizenship.
  8. Any citizen may appeal a decision to the leaders.
  9. New laws and important decisions are published openly.
Territory & Symbols
  1. The capital of New Comland is Hynikin.
  2. New Comland has three provinces: Laver, Avlerta and WayLand.
  3. The cities and towns on the official map are part of New Comland.
  4. Changes to the borders on the official map need the leaders’ approval.
  5. The national flag is the flag shown on this website, and each province keeps its own flag.
  6. Official flags and symbols may not be changed or used to mislead people.
  7. The war flag is flown only on occasions decided by the leaders.
Peace & Diplomacy
  1. New Comland values peace (pasa) and tries to settle disagreements with other nations through talks first.
  2. Only the leaders may declare war or make peace, and any conflict stays non-violent and within the rules of the platforms involved.
  3. The leaders represent New Comland to other nations and micronations.
  4. Embassies (muto-doma) may be set up with the leaders’ approval.
  5. Treaties (kontra) must be published and honored.
  6. Diplomats and guests from other nations must be treated respectfully.
Economy
  1. The currency of New Comland is the Coline.
  2. Colines are a micronational currency and have no guaranteed real-world value.
  3. Counterfeiting Colines or committing fraud with them is forbidden.
  4. Trades and agreements between citizens must be honest and kept.
  5. Only the leaders may create or change the official money.
Language & Culture
  1. Colandian is the language of New Comland.
  2. English words with no Colandian equivalent are part of Colandian until a Colandian word exists.
  3. The official Colandian Dictionary is kept by the leaders, who add new words.
  4. Citizens may suggest new Colandian words.
  5. Other languages and cultures must be respected.
Official Information
  1. Official information about New Comland is published on this website.
  2. Anything claiming to be official that appears elsewhere does not count until the leaders confirm it.
  3. This website may not be used to harm or mislead anyone.
